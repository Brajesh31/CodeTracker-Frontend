# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment
for everyone who participates in CodeTracker, regardless of experience, identity,
background, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give actionable technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing secrets, tokens, or user records.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, doxxing, personal attacks,
sexualized conduct in project spaces, and publication of private information are
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews,
and project-related public spaces when someone is representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact
route in the repository profile. Include relevant context and links. The maintainer
will review reports, protect confidentiality as far as practical, and may edit or
remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation.
